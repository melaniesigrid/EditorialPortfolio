How to sign in

  1. The customer half needs no account at all. Open either quote link and use it as a homeowner would — that is the demo, and it is the half that has to be effortless.
  2. Open the two quote links back to back. Same software, but the questions, the photo prompts, the units and the prices all change with the trade — fence length in linear feet, not roof area in square feet.
  3. For the contractor's dashboard, go to /login and type your email. There is no password — the app emails a one-time link and clicking it signs you in.
  4. The dashboard is invite-only: a link is only issued to an email that already has an account, so an open form cannot be used to create one.

The sign-in is invite-only for a reason, not out of laziness — a passwordless form on a product with no billing is an open invitation to have someone else's inbox used as a relay.

How it works

01

Photograph the job

The customer stands in their own driveway and takes three photos. No measuring tape, no site visit, no phone tag.

02

The AI reads them

It reports the material, the damage, what needs doing, and what it could not tell. It never touches a number.

03

The rate card prices it

Ordinary arithmetic turns those findings into a range using the contractor's own rates. Every adjustment is a visible line.

That split is the whole product. Two contractors with identical photos get different quotes, because they charge different rates — which is what makes the number theirs rather than a market average with their logo on it.

Key achievements

  • 01 The whole product, alone — the customer-facing quote pages, the contractor dashboard, the pricing engine, the database, the design system and the deployment.
  • 02 Drew a hard line between the AI and the money. The model describes what it sees; a pure, tested function does the arithmetic. That is what makes a quote explicable and reproducible instead of a guess that changes each time you ask.
  • 03 Made a failed AI call cost nothing. If the vision step times out or returns nonsense, the lead is still saved, still priced from the customer's own measurements, still emailed — and flagged so the contractor knows to open the photos personally.
  • 04 Made every contractor's brand work on a dark ground. Rather than rejecting colours that vanish against near-black, the code keeps the hue exactly and lifts only the lightness until it clears a legibility floor — still recognisably their colour, guaranteed readable.
  • 05 Fixed a useless answer with a test. The first end-to-end run returned a range of $71,500–$360,000. It now returns $54,000–$144,000 for the same input, and a test caps how wide a range may ever get across every trade.
