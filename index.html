Plate 11

Primordium

Two species of neural network — one hunting, one fleeing — coevolve under the law of the Red Queen. An experiment built so that it can come back null.

  • NEAT-style evolvable topology under a global innovation registry, with compatibility-distance speciation and explicit fitness sharing — no architecture is designed, all of it is mutated into existence.
  • Every synapse carries its own evolvable learning rate, so within-lifetime plasticity is inherited as a rule rather than as weights. Recurrence and memory emerged in 10 runs out of 10.
  • The verdict is a two-sided permutation test over 20,000 shuffles against a frozen-predator control — statistic and threshold both fixed before the first run.

Plate 17

ReconAI

An accounts-payable clerk's afternoon, automated — read the invoice, match every line to the purchase order, and decide whether it is safe to pay.

  • Claude does only what a model should — transcribing the invoice exactly as printed, and matching each line to the right PO line when the wording differs (Wheel stops - replace against Wheel stop replacement), with a confidence score per pairing.
  • The model never does the arithmetic. Every unit-price delta, quantity delta, per-line qty × price, subtotal, tax and total is recomputed in plain TypeScript — the audit is a trust boundary, not a prompt.
  • Returns Approve · Hold · Reject with every mismatch priced, and ships a browser-only sample mode that runs the real reconciliation engine with no API key.

Plate 18

quant-harness

A backtesting harness for AI market agents, built to make the usual self-deceptions structurally impossible rather than merely discouraged.

  • Look-ahead bias is guarded twice — the decision loop makes future bars physically unreachable at decision time, and a detector test truncates the future to prove that earlier steps do not change.
  • Agents are scored against fair baselines, not against zero — all-cash and buy-and-hold controls, a multi-seed driver, and an accounting identity the portfolio must satisfy on every rebalance.
  • Deterministic to the seed and dependency-free at runtime, with 21 passing tests over execution, costs and the honesty guardrails. The report states what the numbers do not prove.

Hello World — I'm Melanie. A Software Developer with a front-end specialty and a love for UX/UI.
